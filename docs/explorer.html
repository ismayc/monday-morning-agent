"You have to establish the run." Change one filter and check.

Take every team in every game, work out what share of its plays were runs, and sort them into four equal groups. How often did each group win? The answer depends on which plays you count, so every filter is listed, in order, with the plays left after it. This is the same output the agent reads. Nothing here is computed in your browser: a script asked the tool every question these buttons can ask and saved what it printed.
