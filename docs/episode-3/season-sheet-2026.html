The same questions as the five-season sheet, asked of this season alone and read against the five complete seasons before it: weeks 1-9, through the games of November 8, 2026. The groups are small and the tool says so wherever it matters.

Does not hold as stated: tried more, made the same

The defined kick was made 68.9% of 1,225 attempts in 2021 to 2025 and 67.3% of 150 in 2026, tried 0.86 and 1.11 times per game. The rate moved down 1.6 points, which is about the same, while the tries per game grew by 29 percent. What changed is how often coaches let their kickers try from that far, not how often the kick goes through.

The verdict follows a rule written in season-sheet.mjs, so you can disagree with it. It compares this season with the five complete seasons before it: under 100 defined kicks in the later row is too few to rule; a rate up 5 points or more holds; a rate within 3 points while tries per game grew by a fifth or more does not hold as stated; a rate within 3 points with flat tries does not hold; a rate down 5 or more is the opposite; between is mixed. Whether kickers are "too good" is not on this sheet. This is the page's rule, not the agent's. Every number is the tool's.

Measured with

"The long field goal, the kick fans mean when they say kickers now make kicks that used to be out of range. In these plays it is any field goal attempt from 50 yards or more, out to the longest attempt on file."

dictionary/long-field-goal.json, written 2026-11-09 by the analyst, episode 3. Rules: a field goal attempt of 50 to 70 yards. Compared with every other field goal attempt, from 18 to 49 yards. Cannot see: the wind, the surface, the snap and the hold, whether a timeout was called to ice the kicker, or the kicks a coach decided not to try, so a rising make rate can come from coaches picking their spots as well as from kickers' legs. A blocked kick counts as a miss.

The name search first: node ./query-plays.mjs --search "field goal" --seasons 2026 found the words in 588 of 23,647 descriptions. Unlike the tush push, the kick has a name in the data; what it does not have is a distance band, and that is what the definition adds.
