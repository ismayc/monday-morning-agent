"You have to establish the run." Five seasons, 2021 to 2025.

Nine questions about establishing the run, each answered by the one tool from every play of 2021 through 2025. Yellow is the fourth of team-games that ran most, blue the fourth that ran least, and the yellow stretch between them is the gap in games won.

Does not hold as stated

Across all games, running early goes with an edge of 6.9 points. But a team that was ahead at halftime ran more in the first half too. Hold the halftime score within 3 points and the edge is -9.0: the teams that ran most early won 46.3%, the teams that ran least 55.3%. Running late goes with a gap of 75.4 points, and teams that are ahead run to use the clock. These plays show no edge for establishing the run; they show one for being ahead.

6.9points early: the first-half gap
-9.0points early, games within 3 at halftime
75.4points late: the second-half gap

The verdict follows a rule written in season-sheet.mjs, so you can disagree with it. It reads the first half of games within 3 points at halftime: under 100 team-games per group is too few to rule; a gap under 5 points does not hold as stated; a gap of 10 or more holds in part; between the two is mixed. This is the page's rule, not the agent's. Every number is the tool's.
